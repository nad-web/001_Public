# Decision Wheel design note

Problem: Small group choices can take more discussion than they deserve.

Audience: Friends deciding what to eat, watch, or do next.

The workspace emphasizes the main task, clear feedback, local records, keyboard/touch controls and readable forms. Runtime files now belong to this app alone. Correct helper logic and content are retained. [App behavior and limits](../README.md) · [Executed verification](../../../docs/VALIDATION.md).
